Each brand works to a **quarterly strategy**: a three-month plan that says what decision the marketing is trying to win, who it's for, how success is measured, and the work month by month. Find it under **Performance → Strategy Plan**.

## What a strategy contains

- **The decision being won:** the buyer and the decision you want them to make.
- **Why this direction:** the reasoning, from the brand's data.
- **What we measure:** how you'll know it's working.
- **The work:** campaigns and content, month by month, including one lead-generation campaign.

It's planned against the brand's credits, so it only commits to work the brand can afford.

## Write a strategy

Click **Write strategy**. You can add an optional focus, for example "win more tender enquiries from municipalities this quarter". Writing takes a few minutes; when it's done, it's sent to the client for [approval](/docs/content/approvals).

You can also ask the Strategist in chat: "write a strategy for next quarter".

Writing a strategy spends the brand's credits.

## Approval

The client approves the strategy like any other request: from the email, the approval page, or by replying. If they ask for changes, the strategy is rewritten with their feedback and sent back.

If the brand's [auto-approval](/docs/strategist/automation) is on, a strategy nobody answers goes ahead when the window ends.

## From strategy to work

Once approved, the strategy's work is built as drafts, and each piece goes through the normal approval process before anything publishes.

## The AI agent's strategies

If your workspace has an [AI agent](/docs/strategist/ai-agent), she can write the next quarter's strategy before the current one ends. When a brand has both, the page shows **the agent's strategy** and **the team's strategy** side by side. Each builds only its own work, and the second is planned knowing what the first already covers.

## How it was built

Owners and agency staff can open **How this strategy was built** on each strategy. It shows who started it, how long it took, the credits charged and planned, and the approval history. Clients don't see this.
