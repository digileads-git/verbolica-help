Everything Verbolica designs, from social images to website pages and lead magnets, is drawn from the brand's identity. Set it up in **Brand Settings → Brand Identity**.

## Read it from the website

The quickest start is **Read your site**. Verbolica reads the brand's website and fills in its colours and fonts, noting which fields it set. Review the result and change anything that's wrong.

## What you can set

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Logos** | The brand mark in its colourways, for light and dark backgrounds. |
| **Colours** | Each colour with its role, such as primary, accent, background or text. A colour can be limited to **Gradients only**, **Small details only** or **Never a background**. |
| **Gradients** | A signature gradient, plus any other approved gradients. Designs use no other gradients. |
| **Fonts** | Typefaces for headings and body text. |
| **Patterns** | Brand textures and patterns, used in images and designs. |
| **Image style** | Directions for photography and illustration, used for every AI image. |
| **Client logos** | Logos of the brand's own customers, which designs may show next to testimonials or case studies. |
| **Design DNA** | The brand's design character: energy, temperature, density, corners, signature move, reference brands, admired sites, and things to **avoid**. |

Not sure how to describe the Design DNA? Click **AI-draft** and Verbolica proposes one from the logo, palette and brand details. Review it before you save.

## Update it from the chat

You can also tell the Strategist about changes, for example "our accent is #F79E2A now", or attach a brand guide PDF and ask it to update the brand. It shows you what will change, and applies it only when you confirm.

## The brand guide

The brand's identity is shown as a **brand guide**: a page with its logos, colours, gradients, fonts, buttons, tone of voice and written guidelines.

To share it with a client, designer or freelancer, click **Share** to create a read-only public link. Turn the link off at any time and it stops working straight away. The public guide leaves out internal details, such as the brand's "avoid" list.

## Handover document

In **Brand Settings → More brand settings → Handover**, generate a document with everything someone would need to take the brand's work elsewhere: strategy, voice, audience and competitive position.
