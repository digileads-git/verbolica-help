## Invite people

Go to **Settings → Team** and invite people by email. Choose a role for each person:

- **Agency** for your own staff.
- **Client** for people at the businesses you work for.

See [Roles and permissions](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

## Assign brands

- **Clients** must be assigned to the brands they should see. They see nothing else.
- **Agency staff** see every brand unless you assign them to specific ones. Once assigned, they see only those.

## Team activity

**Settings → Team → Activity** shows what each team member worked on each day: plans and campaigns started, drafts written, approvals requested and content published. Filter by person and look back up to 90 days. Only owners and agency staff can see it.

## Workspaces

A workspace is your agency or company account. You can belong to several workspaces, with a different role in each, for example your own agency and a partner's.

- **Switch workspace:** open the profile menu and choose the workspace.
- **Create a workspace:** use the profile menu's workspace switcher.
- **Rename or view your plan:** **Settings → Workspace** (owners only).

## Branding

Owners can put the agency's own logo, colours and domain on Verbolica, so clients see your brand rather than ours. See [White-label](/docs/agency/white-label).
