Each brand has its own **credit wallet**: the budget for AI work on that brand. **1 credit is worth $0.01 of AI usage.**

## What spends credits

Work that the AI does for you spends credits, for example:

- writing a blog post, page, social post or email
- planning a campaign or writing a strategy
- designing a website page or lead magnet
- generating images
- keyword and competitor research
- messages to the Strategist in chat

Editing by hand, importing content, approving and publishing don't spend credits.

The balance card on the brand dashboard lists what each action costs.

## Your monthly allowance

Each brand's plan includes a monthly credit allowance, which renews on the **1st of every month**. Billing is per brand, in one subscription for the whole workspace.

## Check your balance

- **Dashboard:** the balance card shows what's left, the brand's plan and the price list.
- **Credit activity:** every charge this period, broken down by action. Open it from the balance card.
- **Chat:** ask the Strategist "how many credits are left?"

When the balance runs low, Verbolica warns you on the dashboard and when you start paid work.

## Top up

Use the **Top up** buttons on the balance card or the **Credit activity** page. Topped-up credits are available straight away.

If a brand runs out, AI work on that brand pauses until you top up or the allowance renews. Nothing already written is affected.

<Callout type="info" title="Trial ended?">
  If your trial has ended, topping up won't help: choose a plan instead. Verbolica tells you which one applies.
</Callout>

Credits are visible to owners and agency staff only. Clients never see them.
