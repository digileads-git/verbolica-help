Verbolica tells you when something needs your attention, such as a draft ready for review, a comment, an approval decision or a publishing problem.

## In the app

The bell icon in the header shows unread notifications. In-app notifications always arrive, whatever your email settings.

## Email

Go to **Settings → Notifications** to choose:

- which types of notification you get by **email**
- whether you get a **daily digest**, a **weekly digest**, or both, and which day the weekly one arrives

Everyone, including clients, can set their own preferences.

## Google Chat

If your workspace uses Google Chat, Verbolica can post to a Chat space: publishing results, ranking changes and engagement spikes. You can also talk to the Strategist from Chat. See [Google Chat](/docs/connections/google-chat).

<Callout title="Not getting emails?">
  Check your spam folder, and check **Settings → Notifications** to make sure the type is switched on.
</Callout>
