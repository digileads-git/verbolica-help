If your team uses Google Chat, you can work with Verbolica without leaving it. Each Chat space is linked to one brand.

<Callout type="info">
  Your Google account's email must match your Verbolica login. If it doesn't, Verbolica tells you it doesn't recognise you.
</Callout>

## Link a space to a brand

Add the Verbolica app to a Google Chat space, then type `/link` and choose the brand.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/drafts` | Drafts waiting for review, with **approve**, **decline** and **improve** buttons. |
| `/report` | A summary of the latest monthly report. |
| `/competitors` | The brand's competitors and the latest gap analysis. |
| `/status` | Which brand the space is linked to, and recent activity. |
| `/link` | Link the space to a different brand. |
| `/unlink` | Disconnect the space from its brand. |
| `/help` | List the commands. |

## Ask the Strategist

Mention the Verbolica app with a question, for example "@Verbolica which posts did best last month?", and the Strategist answers in the space.

## Alerts

Linked spaces can get notifications, such as content published or failed, ranking changes and engagement spikes.
