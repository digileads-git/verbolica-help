Connect a brand's social accounts so approved social posts publish on schedule without copying and pasting.

**Supported platforms:** LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky and Google Business.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Social publishing

Go to **Brand Settings → Connections** and find the **Social publishing** card.

![The Social publishing card, with a button for each platform](/screenshots/social-card.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect a platform

Click the platform's button. A new tab opens where you sign in to that platform and authorise the connection.

</Step>
<Step>

### Refresh

Come back to Verbolica and click **Refresh**. The account appears in the list.

</Step>
</Steps>

You can connect more than one account per platform, for example a personal LinkedIn profile and a company page. Click the platform's button again to add another.

## If a connection stops working

Platforms sometimes expire connections, for example after a password change. If posts stop publishing, open the **Social publishing** card, click **Refresh**, and reconnect any account that shows a problem.

<Callout type="info" title="Post links">
  After a post publishes, Verbolica shows a link to the live post so you can check it and share it with your client.
</Callout>
