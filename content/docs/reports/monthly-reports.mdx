Verbolica builds a report for each brand every month. Reports for the previous month are generated automatically on the 1st, and you can create one for any date range from **Performance → Reports**.

![A monthly report](/screenshots/reports.webp)

## What's in a report

- **Performance summary:** traffic, search clicks and impressions, with month-on-month changes.
- **Narrative summary:** a plain-English account of what happened and why.
- **Engagement:** how deeply people read, from the WordPress plugin's first-party tracking.
- **Rankings:** keywords that moved up and down.
- **Content performance:** how each published piece did in search.
- **Work delivered:** what was produced, by channel, and progress against plan.
- **AI visibility:** whether AI assistants mention the brand.
- **Value scorecard:** the market value of the work delivered, and the outcomes it produced.
- **Recommendations:** what to do next, including content that's slipping or competing with other pages on the same site.

## What clients see

Clients see a version made for them. They see the value of the work delivered as a total, but no internal unit prices, points or rate card.

## Share a report

- **Link:** share a link to the report. It opens without logging in, and you can add a password.
- **PDF:** download a print-ready PDF.
- **Email:** the monthly digest emails the report summary and PDF on the 1st.

## Currency and value rates

Reports use the workspace's reporting currency. The value scorecard uses the brand's **value rates**: the market-rate equivalent of each type of work. Set them in **Brand Settings → More brand settings → Value rates**.

<Callout type="info" title="Reports need data">
  Traffic and search sections need [Google connected](/docs/connections/google-search-console-and-analytics). Engagement needs the [WordPress plugin](/docs/connections/wordpress-plugin).
</Callout>
