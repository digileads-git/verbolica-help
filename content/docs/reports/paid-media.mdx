Find it under **Content → Paid Media**.

## Connect ad accounts

Open **Paid Media → Settings** and connect:

- **Google Ads**, then choose the ad account.
- **Meta Ads**, for Facebook and Instagram advertising.

Campaign data syncs every day.

## Track performance

The Paid Media page lists the brand's campaigns with their spend and results, with a chart over time. Open a campaign to see its keywords, ads and a log of changes.

## Create a campaign

Click **New Campaign** to set up a campaign step by step: platform and type, the campaign basics (name, daily budget, landing page and dates), keywords, then **Review & Launch**. Verbolica can suggest keywords from the brand's search data.

## AI recommendations

**Paid Media → Optimize** lists recommendations to improve campaigns, such as budget changes, keywords to add or exclude, and campaigns to pause. Review each one and apply it if you agree.

## How much the AI may change

In **Paid Media → Settings → AI Automation**, choose an **Automation Mode**:

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Manual** | Every change waits for your approval. |
| **Semi-Auto** | High-confidence budget changes are applied automatically. Pauses and bigger changes wait for approval. |
| **Full Auto** | Every recommendation above the confidence threshold is applied automatically, except removals. |

You can also set a **Confidence Threshold**, a **Budget Cap** per change, and a **Daily Change Limit**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Full Auto changes live campaigns">
  In Full Auto, Verbolica adjusts live campaigns and spend without asking. Agree this with your client, and set a budget cap, before you turn it on.
</Callout>
