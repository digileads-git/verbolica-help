More people now ask AI assistants for recommendations instead of searching. **Performance → AI Visibility** shows whether those assistants mention the brand.

## How it works

Verbolica asks **ChatGPT**, **Gemini** and **Perplexity** the questions the brand's customers ask, and records:

- whether the brand is mentioned
- where it appears in the answer
- which competitors are mentioned instead
- which websites the assistant cited

Checks run twice a month.

## The score

The AI visibility score runs from 0 to 100. It combines:

- **presence** (50%): how often the brand is mentioned
- **share of voice** (30%): mentions compared with competitors
- **position** (20%): how early in the answer the brand appears

## Fixing a gap

Next to every question an assistant answered **without** naming the brand is a **Write answer** button. It creates a draft that answers that question, so the brand has content AI assistants can find and cite. Use **Write all answers** to create drafts for every gap at once.

## Choosing the questions

Manage the list of questions in the questions section at the bottom of the **AI Visibility** page. Add the questions your customers really ask, in their words.

<Callout type="info" title="Not measured is not the same as absent">
  If an assistant answered from memory without searching the web, that answer is marked **not measured** and left out of the score, because it doesn't reflect what's on the web today.
</Callout>
