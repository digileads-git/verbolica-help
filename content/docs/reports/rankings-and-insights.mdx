## Rankings

**Performance → Rankings** tracks where the brand's keywords rank in Google.

- **Visibility chart:** the brand's overall search visibility over time.
- **Keyword table:** each keyword's current position, change and monthly search volume.
- **Decay alerts:** content that has dropped five or more positions, with a prompt to refresh it.
- **Tags:** tag keywords (for example by product or region) and filter the chart by tag.

Rankings are checked automatically every few days. Click **Check Now** for an immediate update (once a day per brand).

## Insights

**Performance → Insights** looks across all the brand's content:

- **Top and under-performers:** which pieces drive results, and which don't.
- **Coverage:** which topics are well covered and which are thin.
- **Cannibalisation alerts:** two or more pages competing for the same keyword, which usually hurts both.
- **Trends:** 90-day sparklines for each page's key metrics.

## Market Gaps

**Performance → Market Gaps** shows topics and keywords competitors rank for that the brand doesn't. Each gap can become a planned topic.

<Callout title="Turn findings into work">
  Ask the Strategist "what should we refresh this month?" and it will pick from decay alerts and cannibalisation, and offer to rewrite the pieces.
</Callout>
