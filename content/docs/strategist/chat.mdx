The Strategist is Verbolica's AI assistant. It knows the brand, its data and every feature in Verbolica, and it can do most tasks for you.

## Where to find it

- **The Chat page** is where you land when you log in. It opens with a briefing on the brand's day, or suggests a first campaign for a new brand.
- **The chat bar** at the bottom of every brand screen opens the same conversation, so you can ask a question without leaving the page you're on.

Past conversations are under **Management → Chat History**.

If your workspace has an [AI agent](/docs/strategist/ai-agent), clients can also reach the Strategist by email.

## What you can ask

**Understand the brand**

- "How is this brand doing this month?"
- "Which articles lost rankings recently?"
- "How many credits are left, and what were they spent on?"

**Plan**

- "Plan next month's search content, focusing on easy wins."
- "Plan an email campaign for the spring launch."
- "Write a strategy for next quarter."

**Write and edit**

- "Write a blog post about choosing a commercial roofing contractor."
- "Make the intro of that post shorter and punchier."
- "Generate five LinkedIn posts from our latest case study."

**Do**

- "Schedule that post for next Tuesday."
- "Add a competitor: example.com."
- "Update our brand guide: our accent colour is now #F79E2A."

You can attach files, such as a brand guide PDF, a Word document or a photo, and ask the Strategist to use them.

Under the message box, **Try:** suggestions show things you can ask. Click one to send it.

## What it won't do without you

Some actions always wait for a person:

- **Designed website pages** are designed in chat but published by clicking on the page, because publishing can overwrite changes made directly in WordPress.
- **Brand guide changes** are shown as a preview first and applied only when you confirm.
- **Sending email** isn't possible: Verbolica exports emails for your own email platform.

## Who can use it

Agency staff and owners can ask the Strategist to do work. Clients can ask questions about their brand, but can't change anything through chat.

<Callout title="Credits">
  Each chat message spends a small number of the brand's credits. Tasks the Strategist carries out, such as writing or designing, spend the same credits as doing them with a button. See [Credits](/docs/account/credits).
</Callout>
