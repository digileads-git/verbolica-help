A brand is one business you create content for. The more Verbolica knows about it, the better everything it writes will be.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the brand

Open the brand switcher and choose **Add brand**. Give it a name and the website address.

</Step>
<Step>

### Work through the setup wizard

Brand Settings walks you through seven steps:

1. **Basics:** name, website, industry and location.
2. **Voice:** how the brand sounds, and words it does or doesn't use.
3. **Personas:** who the brand is talking to and what they care about.
4. **Products:** what it sells, with the selling points that matter.
5. **Competitors:** who it competes with online. Verbolica compares rankings against them.
6. **Connections:** WordPress, Google and social accounts.
7. **Review:** check everything and save.

Every AI feature reads this information, so it is worth getting right. You can change it any time in **Brand Settings**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the website and data

- [Install the WordPress plugin](/docs/connections/wordpress-plugin) so Verbolica can publish and track engagement.
- [Connect Google](/docs/connections/google-search-console-and-analytics) so plans and reports use real search and traffic data.
- [Connect social accounts](/docs/connections/social-accounts) if you will publish social posts.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add brand knowledge (optional, recommended)

In **Brand Settings → More brand settings → Brand knowledge**, add the business's own material: methodology, pricing, positioning, case studies and research. Verbolica searches it every time it writes, so content argues from the brand's real expertise instead of generic industry copy.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The setup checklist

The brand dashboard shows a progress bar for the key steps: WordPress, Search Console, Analytics, personas, products, content pillars and keywords. Work through it until it is complete.

<Callout title="Save before connecting">
  Connections need a saved brand. If a Connect button is greyed out, save the brand first.
</Callout>
