Verbolica is an **AI marketing platform** built for digital marketing agencies and the businesses they work for. AI does the marketing work, from strategy to reporting, and people stay in charge of what goes live:

1. **Brand.** You describe a business once: its voice, audiences, products and competitors.
2. **Strategy.** Verbolica uses search data, your site's performance and what competitors are doing to plan the brand's marketing, quarter by quarter.
3. **Work.** It writes and designs across channels in the brand's voice: search content, social posts, emails, lead magnets and website pages.
4. **Approval.** Clients review and approve work, with comments, by email or inside Verbolica.
5. **Publishing.** Approved work goes to WordPress and social channels on schedule.
6. **Reporting.** Monthly reports show traffic, rankings, engagement and the value of the work delivered.

## The building blocks

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Your agency or company account. It holds your team, your brands and your billing. |
| **Brand** | One business you create content for. Every campaign, draft and report belongs to a brand. |
| **Campaign** | A planned set of content for a channel, such as a month of search articles or a social series. |
| **Draft** | A single piece of content in progress: a blog post, page, social post, email or lead magnet. |
| **Approval request** | A bundle of drafts (or a strategy) sent to the right people for a decision. |
| **Credits** | The brand's AI budget. Writing, designing and researching spend credits. See [Credits](/docs/account/credits). |

## Who uses it

- **Agency owners and staff** set up brands, plan campaigns, write and edit content, and run reporting.
- **Clients** see only the brands they are invited to. They review drafts, approve work, leave comments and read reports.

See [Roles and permissions](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions) for exactly who can do what.

## The Strategist

Every brand has an AI Strategist. You talk to it in the chat on every brand screen. It can explain a brand's performance, suggest what to do next, and do the work itself, from writing a draft to scheduling a post. See [Ask the Strategist](/docs/strategist/chat).
