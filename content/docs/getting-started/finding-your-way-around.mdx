Everything in Verbolica belongs to a brand. Switch brands from the top of the sidebar. The sidebar is grouped into sections:

## Top

- **Chat:** talk to the Strategist. This is where you land when you log in.
- **Dashboard:** the brand at a glance: health score, this week's plan, credits and progress.
- **Client Room:** the client's welcome pack and onboarding checklist. See [Client Room](/docs/for-clients/client-room).

## Content

| Item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Decisions** | Content that helps buyers make a decision: a hub page with supporting pages that answer pricing, comparison and objection questions. See [Decisions campaigns](/docs/content/decisions-campaigns). |
| **Campaigns** | Campaigns that combine several channels. |
| **Search** | SEO content plans, organised by month and topic. |
| **Social** | Social media campaigns and templates. See [Social campaigns](/docs/content/social-campaigns). |
| **Email** | Email campaigns and the email builder. |
| **Paid Media** | Google Ads and Meta Ads performance and recommendations (beta). See [Paid media](/docs/reports/paid-media). |
| **Lead Magnets** | Designed downloadable guides, checklists and scorecards. See [Lead magnets](/docs/content/lead-magnets). |
| **Website** | Designed website pages, built for WordPress and Elementor. See [Website builder](/docs/content/website-builder). |

## Management

| Item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Every draft across every channel, in one list. |
| **Approvals** | Requests sent for a decision, and their status. |
| **Schedule** | A calendar of what will publish and when. Drag to reschedule. |
| **Library** | Everything published, including content imported from the website. |
| **Chat History** | Past conversations with the Strategist. |
| **Brand Settings** | The brand's details, connections, and more settings such as [Automation](/docs/strategist/automation) and brand knowledge. |

## Performance

| Item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Reports** | Monthly client reports. |
| **Insights** | What content performs, and where pieces compete with each other. |
| **Rankings** | Keyword positions over time. |
| **AI Visibility** | Whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity mention the brand. |
| **Market Gaps** | Topics competitors cover that the brand doesn't. |
| **Strategy Plan** | The brand's quarterly strategy. See [Strategy Plan](/docs/brand/strategy-plan). |
| **Activity** | A timeline of everything that happened on the brand. |

Clients see a smaller set of these. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).
