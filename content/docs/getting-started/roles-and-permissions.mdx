Every person in a workspace has one of three roles. A person can belong to more than one workspace, with a different role in each.

| | Owner | Agency | Client |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| See all brands in the workspace | Yes | Yes, unless limited to assigned brands | Only brands they're invited to |
| Plan campaigns and write content | Yes | Yes | No |
| Edit brand settings and connections | Yes | Yes | No |
| Review drafts and leave comments | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Approve or decline work | Yes | On behalf of a client | Yes |
| Read reports | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| See credits, points and fees | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage team, billing and workspace settings | Yes | No | No |

## Limiting agency staff to certain brands

By default, agency staff can see every brand. To limit someone, assign them to specific brands in **Settings → Team**. Once a person has at least one assigned brand, they only see the brands they're assigned to.

## What clients see

Clients see a simplified version of each brand they're invited to: their Client Room, dashboard, the campaigns and drafts the agency has shared, approvals, schedule, library and reports.

Agencies decide what to share. New campaigns start hidden from clients. Use the eye toggle on a campaign to share it.

<Callout type="info" title="Clients never see internal pricing">
  Points, credit costs and other internal pricing are never shown to client users. Reports show clients the value of the work delivered, not your rate card.
</Callout>

To invite people, see [Team and workspaces](/docs/account/team-and-workspaces).
