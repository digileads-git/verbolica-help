Your agency uses Verbolica to plan, write and publish your marketing content. You can see the work for your business, approve it before it goes live, and follow how it performs.

## Logging in

Your agency invites you by email. Follow the link to set your password. After that, log in at the address your agency gave you. If your agency uses its own branding, Verbolica may look like their product rather than ours.

You'll only ever see the brands your agency has given you access to.

## Your first stop: Client Room

The **Client Room** is your welcome pack. It shows your team, upcoming meetings, links to your accounts, and a checklist of the access your agency needs from you. See [Client Room](/docs/for-clients/client-room).

## Reviewing and approving work

When work is ready, you get an email asking for your approval. Open the link; you don't need to log in. For each item you can:

- **Approve** it.
- **Ask for changes**, saying what you'd like changed. Your agency reworks it and sends it back.
- **Decline** it, saying why. Nothing is changed and your agency is told.

You can also highlight any passage and leave a comment on it.

Everything waiting for you is also listed under **Approvals** when you log in.

<Callout type="warn" title="Check the deadline">
  Your agency may have agreed that work goes ahead if nobody responds within a set number of days. The approval email says if a request will pass on its own, and when.
</Callout>

## Seeing what's planned and published

- **Schedule:** a calendar of what's going out and when.
- **Library:** everything that's been published.
- **Campaigns and drafts:** the work your agency has shared with you.

## Reports

Each month your agency can share a report showing traffic, rankings, engagement and the work delivered. Find it under **Reports**, or open the link your agency emails you.

## Recommendations

Your agency may share suggestions from Verbolica's Strategist with you. They appear on your dashboard under **Recommended by your team**. Approve one to have the work started, or decline it.

## Notifications

Choose which emails you receive in **Settings → Notifications**.
