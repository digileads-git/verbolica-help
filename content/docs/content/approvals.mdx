Approvals are how clients sign off work before it goes live. An approval request can hold one draft, many drafts, a list of topics, or a strategy.

## Send work for approval

Select the drafts you want reviewed and click **Request approval**. The dialog lists the brand's approvers:

- **Main approvers** are always included and can't be unticked.
- Tick anyone else who should review.

Everyone ticked gets an email with a link to review and decide. They don't need to log in.

## What approvers can do

For each item, an approver can:

- **Approve** it, so it moves on to scheduling and publishing.
- **Ask for changes**, saying what to change. The team reworks it and sends it back.
- **Decline** it, saying why. Nothing is changed and the team is told.

Approvers can also highlight passages and comment on them.

Agency staff can record a decision a client gave elsewhere, for example on a call, with **Approve on behalf of client** or **Decline on behalf of client**.

## Who the approvers are

Set approvers on the brand's **Automation** page, in the **Approvers** card. Client users on the brand can always approve. You can add team members as approvers, and mark anyone as a **main approver**.

## Reminders and silence

If nobody answers, a reminder goes out after **3 days**.

What happens next depends on the brand's **What silence means** setting on the **Automation** page:

- **Approvals pass on their own** is off: requests wait until someone decides.
- **Approvals pass on their own** is on: a request nobody answers is approved after the number of days you set (0 to 30, 5 by default). The approval history marks it as passed on silence.

<Callout type="warn" title="Silence can publish">
  With auto-approval on, unanswered work can go live without anyone reading it. Agree the setting with your client before you turn it on.
</Callout>

## Decisions are permanent

Once a request is decided, it can't be edited. This keeps an honest record of who approved what and when. To change approved work, edit the draft and send a new request.

## Off-platform work

Use a **custom approval request** to get sign-off on things Verbolica didn't produce, such as a design file or an ad campaign.
