Verbolica plans, writes and designs emails. It **does not send them**. When an email is ready, you download its HTML and load it into the email platform you already use, such as Mailchimp, Klaviyo or HubSpot.

## Plan and write

Under **Content → Email**, create a campaign. Ask Verbolica to generate an email plan, or add emails yourself. Write each email like any other draft, and send it for [approval](/docs/content/approvals).

## Design an email

On an email draft, click **Design email** to open the email builder:

- **Blocks** on the left: heading, text, image, button, two columns, divider, spacer and social links.
- **Live preview** in the centre.
- **Settings** for the selected block on the right.

The builder starts from your copy: headings become heading blocks, paragraphs become text, and a short standalone link becomes a button. Changes save automatically. If you edit the copy later, click **Re-layout** to rebuild the design from it.

## Export

Click **Preview email** to check it, then **Download HTML**. The file is built to display reliably across email apps, including Outlook. It contains an `{{unsubscribe}}` placeholder: replace it with your email platform's unsubscribe tag.

<Callout type="info">
  The email builder uses the brand's colours. Fonts fall back to web-safe fonts, because most email apps can't load custom fonts.
</Callout>
