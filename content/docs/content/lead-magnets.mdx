A **lead magnet** is something valuable a visitor downloads in exchange for their details: a guide, checklist, scorecard or ebook. Verbolica writes it and designs it as a finished PDF in the brand's style.

Find them under **Content → Lead Magnets**.

## Create a lead magnet

Click **New lead magnet** and choose how to start:

- **From your brand knowledge:** describe **what it should cover, and who for**, and click **Write it**. Verbolica writes it from the brand's own material, so it reflects the business's real expertise.
- **Your copy, just designed:** paste in content you've already written and click **Add it**. Use headings to split it into chapters; without headings it comes in as one section.

Read it through and edit it like any other draft before you design it.

<Callout title="Add brand knowledge first">
  Lead magnets written from brand knowledge are only as good as that knowledge. Add the business's methodology, case studies and research in **Brand Settings → More brand settings → Brand knowledge** first.
</Callout>

## Design it as a PDF

On the lead magnet, click **Design it** and choose:

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Format** | **On-screen (landscape)** for reading on a screen, or **A4 (print)** or **US Letter (print)** for printing. |
| **Creativity** | **Classic** (clean and corporate), **Balanced** (designed editorial) or **Expressive** (art-directed). |
| **Brief** | Optional direction, for example "one big photo per chapter". |

Designing takes a few minutes and carries on if you leave the page. When it's done, the PDF is ready to download.

Want a different look? Click **Redesign** and pick another format or creativity level. The words are kept, so you only pay for the design again.

## Lead magnets in Decisions campaigns

[Decisions campaigns](/docs/content/decisions-campaigns) suggest **decision kit ideas**: lead magnets that help buyers decide. Click **Generate** on an idea to write it. For tools like calculators and estimators, Verbolica writes a specification for a developer to build instead.
