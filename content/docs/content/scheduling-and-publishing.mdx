## Publish to WordPress

Approved drafts publish to the brand's WordPress site through the [Verbolica Connect plugin](/docs/connections/wordpress-plugin). You can:

- **Publish now:** send it to the site straight away.
- **Schedule for later:** pick a date. Verbolica checks for scheduled content every hour and publishes it when its date arrives.

Posts publish with their categories, tags, featured image, author and SEO title and description.

## The Schedule calendar

**Management → Schedule** shows every scheduled item on a month calendar, colour-coded by status. Drag an item to a new day to reschedule it. You can't schedule into the past.

![The Schedule calendar](/screenshots/schedule.webp)

The **Keep publishing to weekdays** setting on the **Automation** page stops automatic scheduling from landing on weekends.

## Changing published content

If you edit a draft after it's published, the draft shows that it's out of sync with the website. Click **Update on WordPress** to push the change.

If the brand requires approval for live updates, you'll be asked to request approval instead, and the change goes live once it's approved.

## Social posts

Approved social posts publish to the brand's [connected social accounts](/docs/connections/social-accounts) at their scheduled time. Once a post is live, Verbolica shows a link to it.

## Email

Verbolica doesn't send email. Emails are designed in Verbolica and exported as HTML for your own email platform. See [Email campaigns](/docs/content/email-campaigns).

## If something fails

Failed publishes show on the draft with the reason. The most common causes are a firewall blocking Verbolica or an outdated plugin. See [WordPress publishing problems](/docs/troubleshooting/wordpress-publishing-problems).
