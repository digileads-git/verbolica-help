Verbolica writes **video scripts** in the brand's voice like any other draft. If the brand has a HeyGen account, it can also turn a script into a talking-head video.

## Connect HeyGen

<Steps>
<Step>

### Get an API key

In your HeyGen account, copy your API key.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add it to the brand

In **Brand Settings → Connections**, paste the key into the **HeyGen Video** card and save. Choose the avatar and voice to use.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Make a video

Open a video script draft, check the script, and generate the video. Video generation spends the brand's credits, priced by length. See [Credits](/docs/account/credits).
