Find social media under **Content → Social**.

## Start a campaign

Click **Start Social** and fill in:

- **Campaign name** and **Target month**.
- **Platforms:** Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest or Bluesky.
- **Executive Profiles:** post as a named person, such as the founder, in their own voice. Set these up in **Brand Settings → Executives**.
- **Focus:** tie the campaign to a content pillar, a product, or another campaign.
- **How many posts?:** from 6 to 30, or **0 — Blank Campaign** to fill it yourself.
- **Brief:** the goals, key messages and audience.

Click **Generate Campaign**. Verbolica plans the post ideas; click **Generate all posts** to write them.

You can also build a social campaign from a [Decisions campaign](/docs/content/decisions-campaigns), or a blended campaign that combines Search and social.

## Work with posts

On a campaign page you can:

- edit each post's caption and hashtags
- drag posts into a different order
- add your own post with **Add Custom Post**: give a topic for Verbolica to write, or switch on **Write the content myself**
- pick a different image template for one post
- choose which connected account a post goes out on, for example a company page instead of a personal profile
- show or hide the campaign from the client with the eye icon

## Images and carousels

Each post gets an image in the brand's style, made from the brand's **social image templates**. On LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook, a post can be a **carousel** of several images: add, remove and reorder slides on the post card.

To set up templates, open **Social → Templates**:

- **Design a set from this brand:** Verbolica designs a set of templates from the brand's colours, fonts and logo.
- **Upload PNG:** upload your own template designs.
- **Brand defaults per platform:** choose which template each platform uses by default. Choose **No default** to skip images for a platform.

![Social image templates and brand defaults per platform](/screenshots/social-templates.webp)

## Publish

Send posts for [approval](/docs/content/approvals) first. Once approved, click **Publish** on a post and choose **Publish now** or **Schedule for later**. Posts go out through the brand's [connected social accounts](/docs/connections/social-accounts).

After a post goes live, a **View post** link takes you to it on the platform. If a platform rejects a post, it's marked as failed with the reason, and you're notified.
