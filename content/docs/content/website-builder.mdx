The website builder designs website pages in the brand's style and publishes them to the brand's WordPress site. Pages can publish as native **Elementor** content, so the client can keep editing them in Elementor.

Find it under **Content → Website**.

![The Website page, with Match your existing site and Add page](/screenshots/website.webp)

**Before you start:** connect the site with the [WordPress plugin](/docs/connections/wordpress-plugin), and fill in the brand's identity (colours, fonts, logo and image style) in **Brand Settings**. The more complete the brand, the closer the designs land.

## Design a page

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add the page

Click **Add page**, give it a **Title** and choose a **Page type**. Optionally choose a page to publish it under, so it sits at an address like `/services/this-page/`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give it its words

Every page needs copy. You can:

- type the words, or a list of points the page must make, into the copy box
- click **Write the copy for me** to have Verbolica draft it from the brand's details, personas and brand knowledge
- start from an existing page draft: open the draft and click **Design this page**

To use your copy word for word, tick **Keep my copy exactly as written**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the brief

All optional:

- **Theme:** light, dark, or automatic from the brand.
- **Creativity:** from **Classic** (clean and conventional) to **Expressive** (bold type, colour-blocking, motion).
- **Page depth:** **Short** (one argument), **Standard** or **Deep** (thorough and referenceable).
- **Audience** and **Must include:** who the page is for, and the offers, features or proof it has to contain.
- **Match the layout of:** follow another page's structure. **Automatic** follows a sibling page under the same parent.

</Step>
<Step>

### Design it

Verbolica designs the page. Review it in the editor, then refine it.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Design the home page first">
  The first page you design sets the style the other pages follow, so start with the home page. Choose **Design fresh** on any page that should ignore the site style.
</Callout>

## Refine the design

In the page editor you can:

- **Chat:** ask for changes in plain English, for example "make the hero shorter" or "add a testimonials section". You can attach a screenshot of a layout you like.
- **Edit text on the page:** click any text to edit it, with a toolbar for headings, bold, lists, alignment and links.
- **Content:** edit all the page's text in one panel, grouped by section.
- **Improve copy:** rewrite the words without touching the design.
- **Polish:** review the page for layout and readability problems, such as low contrast, and fix them.
- **Images:** generate an on-brand image, upload your own or point at a URL.
- **Insert a form:** place a form from the site's form plugin, such as Gravity Forms, Elementor Forms or Contact Form 7.
- **History:** go back to an earlier version.

## Get approval

Click **Approval** to send the design to the client before it goes live. The page can't publish while it's waiting on a decision.

## Publish

Click **Publish to your live site**. Use the arrow beside it to choose how it publishes:

- **Verbolica page:** the design exactly as designed.
- **Native Elementor:** converted into real Elementor sections and widgets, so the client can edit every heading, button and image in Elementor.

Other publishing options:

- **Include the site's header & footer:** keep it on for a normal page; turn it off for a standalone landing page.
- **Search visibility:** choose whether search engines may index the page.
- **Replace a page:** publish over an existing page on the site instead of creating a new one.

Images are uploaded to the site's own media library, so the live page doesn't depend on Verbolica.

<Callout type="warn" title="Republishing overwrites Elementor edits">
  Publishing again replaces the page in WordPress. Any changes made directly in Elementor since the last publish are lost. Once the client is editing a page in Elementor, make further changes there.
</Callout>

## Header, footer and other templates

On a site with **Elementor Pro**, the **Site templates** card designs the site's header (including mega menus), footer, 404 page, search results and blog templates in the same style.

## See the whole site

Click **Sitemap** to see the live site's pages as a map, showing which pages are designed and live, designed but not live, or not designed yet.
