Content in Verbolica is organised by **channel**, and within each channel by **campaign**.

## Channels

| Channel | Sidebar | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Search** | Content → Search | Blog posts and web pages planned around keywords and topics. |
| **Decisions** | Content → Decisions | A hub page and supporting pages that help buyers decide. See [Decisions campaigns](/docs/content/decisions-campaigns). |
| **Social** | Content → Social | Posts for each social platform, with branded images and carousels. See [Social campaigns](/docs/content/social-campaigns). |
| **Email** | Content → Email | Email campaigns, designed in the email builder and exported for your email platform. |
| **Lead magnets** | Content → Lead Magnets | Designed downloadable PDFs such as checklists, scorecards and guides. See [Lead magnets](/docs/content/lead-magnets). |
| **Website** | Content → Website | Designed website pages for WordPress and Elementor. See [Website builder](/docs/content/website-builder). |

## Campaigns

A campaign is a planned set of content, usually for one month or one goal. You can:

- **Let Verbolica plan it.** Generate a plan and Verbolica proposes topics using keyword data, your site's performance and competitor gaps.
- **Fill it by hand.** Every campaign lets you add your own topics or paste in finished copy.
- **Mix both.** Start from a generated plan, then add, remove or rename items.

Search campaigns can also **import Word documents**. Each `.docx` file becomes a draft page with the client's exact copy, headings, lists, links and tables included.

## Standalone content

Not everything belongs to a campaign. One-off pieces live in standalone cards on the **Overview** page, one per channel.

## Tags

On a campaign page, use the **Tag** button on any item to add your own labels, such as "Keep", "Needs work" or "Old". A filter bar above the list narrows it to items with the tags you pick.

## Sharing with clients

Campaigns start **hidden** from clients. When a campaign is ready for the client to see, click its eye icon to share it.
