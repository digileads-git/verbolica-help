**Lead generation** gives your agency public pages where prospects get something valuable, such as an audit of their own website, in exchange for their details. Every request becomes a lead for your team to follow up.

It's part of the agency tools, available in workspaces with the agency add-on. Open it from **Settings → Lead generation**.

## The lead magnets

| Lead magnet | What the prospect gets |
|---|---|
| **Content Audit** | A free SEO content audit of their site, with a three-month content strategy. |
| **Growth Audit** | A self-serve audit of their site's findability, foundations, commercial clarity and technical health, generated and emailed to them. See [Growth Audits and proposals](/docs/agency/growth-audits-and-proposals). |

Each starts switched **off**. Click **Settings** on a lead magnet, where you can:

- switch the public page on or off
- set its web address (its slug), for example `/growth-audit/your-agency`
- set the calls to action shown with the result, such as **Book a demo**
- add your proof: up to four case studies with verified numbers, and a client testimonial

The pages carry your agency's branding.

## Work email only

Prospects must give a work email address; Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and similar addresses are refused. Results are sent by email, so a prospect has to give a real address to see them.

Each lead a prospect generates spends credits, because the audit is AI work.

Growth Plans and audits you send to a named prospect aren't public lead magnets; they're on [Proposals](/docs/agency/growth-audits-and-proposals).

## Leads

Click **Leads** on a lead magnet, or **All leads**, to list every request in one place: who, their company and website, which lead magnet they used, and whether they've opened the result. Filter by lead magnet to focus on one.
