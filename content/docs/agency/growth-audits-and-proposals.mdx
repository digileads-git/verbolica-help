A **Growth Audit** is a sales pitch built from a live audit of a prospect's website. It's designed to earn a meeting: it shows, honestly, where the prospect is losing out and what your agency would do about it. Find audits under **Proposals → Audits**.

## Create an audit

<Steps>
<Step>

### Enter the prospect

Enter the **Prospect website**, and optionally the prospect's name and the **Competitors to compare against**. Leave competitors blank and Verbolica finds them.

</Step>
<Step>

### Generate

Click **Generate audit**. Verbolica checks the site's foundations, technical health and speed, search visibility, competitors and how AI assistants see the brand, then scores it and writes it up. It takes a few minutes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Review and publish

The audit starts as a draft that only your workspace can see. Read it, then click **Publish** to make the link live and send it to the prospect.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What the prospect sees

- an **overall growth score** out of 100, with the three biggest numbers behind it
- sections for **Search & Visibility**, **Foundation & Technical** and **Conversion & Commercial**, worst first, each with findings and quick wins
- how they compare with competitors, and where they rank on shared keywords
- **How AI engines see you:** what ChatGPT and Gemini know about the brand
- a full-length view of their site on a phone
- one of your case studies and your call to action

The page carries your agency's branding and is hidden from search engines.

<Callout type="info" title="Honest by design">
  The audit only reports what the checks found. It credits real strengths and never invents a problem, so you can send it with confidence.
</Callout>

## Track interest

The audits list shows how many times each published audit has been viewed, so you know when to follow up. **Unpublish** hides the page again.

## Growth Plans and proposals

**Proposals** also holds **Growth Plans**: a plan for a prospect's growth that you can send as a proposal. A proposal can include an accept button, so the prospect accepts it on the page. You're emailed as soon as they do, and the accepted version is kept on record.
