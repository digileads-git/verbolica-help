Workspaces with the agency add-on can run Verbolica under their own brand. Clients then see your logo and colours when they log in, open approvals or read reports.

## Branding

Workspace owners set this in **Settings → Agency branding**:

- your **logo** and **icon**
- your **colours**

![Settings → Agency branding](/screenshots/agency-branding.webp)

Verbolica builds a complete theme from them for the app, the Client Room, approval pages and reports. Your colours are adjusted where needed so text stays readable.

## Your own domain

In **Settings → Custom domain**, connect a domain you own, such as `app.youragency.com`, so clients use your address instead of app.verbolica.com. Follow the steps on the page to add the DNS record.

## What clients see

With branding set, client-facing pages use your brand: the app itself, the Client Room, approval pages, shared reports and your [lead generation](/docs/agency/lead-generation) pages.
