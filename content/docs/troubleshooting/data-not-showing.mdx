title: Data not showing
description: Why traffic, search, ranking or engagement numbers might be missing or look wrong.
icon: LifeBuoy
---

## Search and traffic numbers are empty

- **Is Google connected?** Check **Brand Settings → Connections**. The Google card should show **Connected** with both a Search Console property and a GA4 property saved. See [Connect Google](/docs/connections/google-search-console-and-analytics).
- **Is it the right property?** If you have several properties, make sure the saved ones match the brand's website.
- **Has it just been connected?** Data arrives in the daily update. Give it a day.

## The last few days are missing

Google reports Search Console data two to three days late. This is normal, and it fills in over the following days.

## Old data is ignored

Verbolica treats search and traffic data older than 90 days as out of date when planning and writing, so decisions reflect what's happening now. Reports still show historical periods.

## Engagement numbers are empty

Engagement (reading time, scroll depth, clicks and form submissions) comes from the [WordPress plugin](/docs/connections/wordpress-plugin), not from Google Analytics. Check that the plugin is installed, active and connected. Numbers build up as people visit.

## Rankings look out of date

Rankings update automatically every few days. Click **Check Now** on the **Rankings** page for an immediate check (once a day per brand).

## Numbers differ from Google's own reports

Small differences are normal. Google Analytics and Search Console count differently from each other, and both adjust recent figures after the fact. Compare the same date range, and allow for the reporting delay.
